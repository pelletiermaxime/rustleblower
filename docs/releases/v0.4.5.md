Missed permission notifications now open the exact connection details in
History. You can see which fallback action applied even when your saved History
filters hide the event. An unanswered prompt defaults to Deny once.

The Linux x86-64 AppImage includes in-app updates. If you are upgrading from
v0.4.3 or earlier, install this AppImage manually. To download future stable
releases in the background, open **Settings → Updates** and enable automatic
checks and AppImage downloads for Rustleblower with prereleases off.

Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS`. Linux packages require glibc 2.39 or
newer, a compatible graphics driver, and Wayland or X11. Without FUSE, launch
the AppImage with `--appimage-extract-and-run`.
